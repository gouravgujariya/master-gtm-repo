PHASE 01

Strategy — decide who & why

Orchestrated by cold-email-kickoff · nothing gets sent until this is done.

1.1

Onboard the client → ICP

Deep-research the client's website and every sub-page, pull the case studies / existing customers, and combine with the client questionnaire. Produces client-profile.yaml (ICP, offer, stack).

icp-onboarding FireCrawl · MCP questionnaire
1.2

Pick the offer / lead magnet

Choose the free hook the emails lead with — the reason a stranger replies. Maps to one value-prop category: make money, save time, save money, or reduce risk.

lead-magnet-brainstorm
1.3

Build the campaign strategy

Generate 15–25 campaign angles, ordered broad → niche, each with a targeting filter, an AI-personalization approach, and a value prop. Three are always included:

always Creative Ideas· New Hire· Lookalike pick ONE to launch first — usually Creative or Lookalike
campaign-strategy
Runs in parallel · ~2–3 weeks lead time
∥

Stand up the sending infrastructure

Buy domains, create inboxes, warm them, and export to the sender. This runs alongside strategy because warmup takes two weeks — start early.

1Porkbun · domains→ 2Inboxkit / Maildoso · inboxes + warmup→ 3Export → Plusvibe 2-week warmup · 30 emails/inbox/day
inbox-infrastructure
PHASE 02

Build the list — verified & sendable

Turn one campaign angle into a clean contact list. Waterfalls stop at the first hit; nothing unverified passes.

2.1

Source the companies

Bulk data first — we already own ~12.3M US businesses and ~900K SaaS companies locally. Go live only if that isn't enough, adapting the tool to the campaign.

waterfall 1Bulk 12M / 900K CSV→ 2AI Ark · lookalikes→ 3Apify · hiring/LinkedIn→ 4Serper · local
bulk-list ai-ark Apify · MCP serper
2.2

Find the decision-makers + emails

For each company, find the right person and their email. Waterfall by cost — stop as soon as one returns a usable email; escalate to ColdIQ only when stuck.

waterfall 1AgenticData→ 2AI Ark→ 3ColdIQ · last resort then rank by title + seniority
AgenticData · MCP ai-ark coldiq select-contacts
2.3

Verify every email — the gate

Deliverability is the priority. Every email runs through OptimaVerifier before it can be sent.

2.4

Grade the list before upload

Score the list on verification coverage, duplicates, title diversity, and ICP fit. Fix or re-pull anything that grades poorly — a bad list wastes warmed inboxes.

list-quality-scorecard
PHASE 03

Copy & launch

Write copy that earns replies. Upload as a draft — a human always hits Start.

3.1

Write the sequence

Situation → value + proof → low-effort CTA. A 4-email sequence that rotates the value-prop angle, 50–90 words each, research as the personalization. QA for deliverability, then serialize to variants.yaml.

campaign-copywriting email-spintax spam-word-checker
3.2

Upload to the sender — as DRAFT

Push verified leads + copy into Plusvibe, auto-selecting the healthiest inboxes (least-recently-used). The campaign lands in DRAFT — you review in the UI and hit Start yourself.

campaign-upload Plusvibe / Smartlead
PHASE 04

Measure & iterate

Judge on positive replies, not opens. Kill losers, scale winners, keep a weekly rhythm.

4.1

Wait 21 days, then score positive replies

The north-star metric is positive replies ÷ total sent — not raw reply rate. Reply to interested prospects within seconds; they convert far better.

positive-reply-scoring
4.2

Design the next experiment

Change one variable at a time — list or copy, not both — so you learn what actually moved the number. Winners (≥2× baseline) scale; losers get killed.

experiment-design
4.3

Keep the weekly rhythm

Mon deliverability audit · Wed positive-reply sweep · Fri retrospectives on any campaign hitting day 21. Consistency is what separates the top 1%.

cold-email-weekly-rhythm

Live tools

Non-negotiable principles

The rules every campaign obeys, regardless of tooling.

  • 1%A healthy domain sustains ≥1% reply rate after 200 sends.
  • ★Positive reply rate is the north star — right replies, not just replies.
  • ✎Always upload as DRAFT. A human hits Start.
  • ⏱2-week warmup before sending · 21-day window before judging.
  • ✓Never send an unverified email. OptimaVerifier is the gate.
  • ⚖B2B only · honor unsubscribes · real physical address.
